# BMD Portal

BMD (Birth, Marriage, and Death) Portal is a digital governance platform designed to simplify the application, verification, and management of vital civil certificates. The system enables citizens to apply for Birth, Marriage, and Death certificates online, upload required documents, track application progress, and receive verified certificates through a secure and transparent process.

The platform aims to reduce paperwork, minimize manual visits to government offices, and improve the efficiency of public service delivery. By digitizing certificate registration and verification workflows, BMD enhances accessibility, accuracy, and transparency for both citizens and government authorities.

## Key Features

-   Online application for Birth, Marriage, and Death certificates
-   Secure document submission and management
-   Real-time application status tracking
-   Certificate verification and validation workflows
-   Role-based access for citizens and administrative authorities
-   Digital record management and streamlined processing
-   User-friendly and responsive interface

## Objective

The primary objective of the BMD Portal is to modernize civil registration services by providing a centralized digital platform that ensures faster processing, improved transparency, reduced administrative burden, and convenient access to essential government services.
